# Workshop & Spare Parts Inventory

This file lists the workshop tools, consumables, and spare components available for designing and prototyping Kiwi. For active robot hardware specifications and pinouts, see [`hardware.md`](hardware.md).

## Tools

### Electronics
- Basic electronics tools: soldering iron, pliers, multimeter.
- No oscilloscope (planned DIY microcontroller-based scope), no function generator.

### Mechanics
- Full metalworking (angle grinder, welder, drill press) and woodworking (circular saw, table saw, planer...) workshop.
- **BambuLab A1** 3D printer with PLA, PETG (and potentially TPU).

### Computing & Networking
- Windows 10 PC with SolidWorks Student Edition 2026, Linux and macOS with JetBrains Student Pack 2026 (RustRover, CLion, PyCharm...).
- Networking gear (router, Ethernet cables, Internet access).

---

## Consumables

### Electronics
- Connectors (JST, JST-SM, XT60, Dupont...), wires, Wago Lever-Nuts.
- USB cables.

### Mechanics
- M2 to M8 fasteners, heat-set threaded inserts, nylon standoffs/spacers.
- Threadlocker.
- T-slot aluminum extrusions (already mounted on the robot chassis).
- Steel and aluminum offcuts.

## Component Stock

### Electronics
- STM32 boards: **NUCLEO-L432KC**, **NUCLEO-G401**, **NUCLEO-F767ZI** (mounted on Kiwi).
- **Raspberry Pi 4B 4GB** (mounted on Kiwi).
- Two older all-in-one PCs.
- 3x 5A buck converters.
- 2x **6S LiFePO4 288Wh** batteries + 4x **4S LiPo 1500mAh** batteries.
- 24V 15A regulated LED power supply.
- LiDARs: **YDLidar G6** (mounted on Kiwi), **RPLidar C1**, and one generic mini LiDAR.
- Multiple ToF (Time-of-Flight) sensors.
- MPU Inertial Measurement Unit (IMU).
- 720p webcam.
- Dual 3A H-bridge.
- Arduino UNO boards and assorted prototyping sensor modules.
- Finder 24V relay.
- Logic level shifters (transistor-based and 3x `74LVC245`).

### Electromechanical
- 5x **BLM3650** brushless motors with planetary gearbox and integrated ESC.
- 2x bare **BLM3650** brushless motors with integrated ESC.
- 2x yellow hobby DC motors with encoders.
- Longer NEMA17 stepper motors (4 standard, 1 short, 1 long).
- 2x BIB J30 appliance pumps.
- ~20x 9g 180° hobby servos.

### Mechanical
- 4x omni/holonomic wheels (3 mounted on Kiwi).
- 4x Mecanum wheels.
- 2x yellow hobby wheels.
- Bearings, GT2 pulleys and belts.
- Heavy rubber tracks.
