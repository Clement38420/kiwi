Kiwi GCS

A ground control station that is clear, precise and a little playful. A three-wheeled kiwi robot: vivid green for identity, graphite for rigor, room to breathe everywhere. Designed light-first, with a full dark mode.

1. Logo

A rounded kiwi triangle, three wheels at the tips, a central eye. The whole mark is tilted 12° to suggest movement. The yellow dot in the eye is the brand’s only warm touch.

Primary

On a light background. Graphite wheels, kiwi triangle.

Dark

Light wheels, lime triangle. Dark mode uses it automatically.

Monochrome

One tint for print, stamps and engraving.

App icon

Wordmark

Kiwi GCS

Space Grotesk 700. “GCS” in the kiwi text color.

Sizes

Minimum: 16 px (symbol), 96 px wide (wordmark).

Do

  • Leave clear space around the logo equal to one wheel’s thickness (≈ 1/5 of its height).
  • Use the dark version on any dark background.
  • Keep the eye yellow, except in monochrome.

Avoid

  • Stretching, rotating differently or removing a wheel.
  • Recoloring the wheels green or yellow.
  • Placing the logo on a green background or a busy photo.

2. Colors

Each color has a role in both modes. Kiwi green stays the same; the neutrals, the text green and the states adapt.

Brand

Interface neutrals

States (link, GPS, battery, alerts)

Reserved for the system’s real status. Warning uses orange so it is never confused with the brand’s yellow spark.

Distribution

Neutrals 85 %Kiwi 14 %

Spark ≈ 1 %: a single warm dot per screen (“robot active” indicator, loading, empty state). Never for an alert, never as text.

Contrast

  • Green text: use kiwi text, never the vivid kiwi on white.
  • On a kiwi or spark background: graphite text #0F172A.
  • States are never carried by color alone: add an icon or a label.

Dark mode

  • Midnight-blue backgrounds, not pure black: less eye strain on night flights.
  • No pure white: text is #F1F5F9.
  • Surfaces are told apart by tint and a thin outline, not by shadow.

4. Components in light and dark

The same components, the same variables. Only the theme changes. The button at the top of the page switches the whole guide.

5. Shapes and spacing

Spacing (4 px base)

4 · 8 · 12 · 16 · 24 · 32. Panels: 16 px inner padding.

Radii

81216pill

Borders and shadows

1 px border everywhere. Light: very soft shadow 0 2px 8px at 6 % black. Dark: no shadow, just the border.
